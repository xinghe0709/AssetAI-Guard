    01 · Context

    What problem are we solving?

    Engineering teams still reconcile load limits manually across PDFs, drawings, and spreadsheets. That slows operations, increases compliance risk, and makes real-time safety decisions harder.

    02 · Solution

    How AssetGuard works

    • AI document extraction PDFs and drawings → structured assets and load criteria
    • Evaluation engine Compare live loads to thresholds and log every assessment
    • Operations dashboard Assets, alerts, and history in one place

    03 · Architecture

    Three components, one workflow

    gjp-assetguard-extraction-tool runs AI extraction; AssetGuard-AI exposes APIs and stores business data; assetguard-ui delivers the operator experience.
