Introduction

What we are presenting

A decision-support platform for GJP Engineering — compare on-site equipment loads against design limits with a clear audit trail.

  • Client — GJP Engineering (structural / load compliance)
  • Team — UWA CITS5206, Group 11
  • Scope — Web app, API, AI extraction pipeline, documentation
Summary

Problem vs Solution

Client problem

  • Design limits scattered in PDFs — hard to use on site
  • Manual load checks are slow and error-prone
  • No central record of outcomes and alerts

Our solution

  • Web platform + AI pipeline (PDF/images → JSON criteria)
  • Role-based UI: evaluate loads, manage assets, import data
  • Logged results and email alerts when non-compliant

Decision-support only — not a replacement for engineering judgement.
